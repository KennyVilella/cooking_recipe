.. _pancake:

Pancake
=======

Ingredient List
---------------

- 2 eggs
- 150 ml of milk
- 125 g of flour
- 1 teaspoon of baking powder
- 2 tablespoon of sugar or vanilla sugar
- 1 pinch of salt

Steps
-----

1. Mixed flour, baking powder, sugar and salt
2. In a second bowl, mixed the two eggs yolk with the milk
3. Mixed the contents of the two bowls until homogeneous
4. Rest the mixture for 15-30min
5. Whisk the egg whites until they form peaks, and add delicately to the previous mixture
6. Cook the pancake in a pan with a bit of butter or oil
7. When there are bubbles its time to turn the pancake
