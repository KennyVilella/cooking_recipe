Ricotta meatball
================

Ingredient List
---------------

- 400g tomato in can
- 500g minced meat, beef if possible
- 100g of chapelure or breadcrumb
- 250g of ricotta
- 1 egg
- [optional] 10-30g of parmesan
- [optional] 10g parsley
- [optional] 5-15g of oregano
- [optional] tomato paste

Steps
-----

1. Mix egg and chapelure
2. Add ricotta, parmesan, oregano, parsley, salt, black pepper and meat
3. Make some meatball
4. Make the meatball brown with some olive oil, then remove
5. Put tomato sauce, cook a bit until reduce
6. Eventually add tomato paste, and add the meatball.
