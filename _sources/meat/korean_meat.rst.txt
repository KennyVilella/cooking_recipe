Korean style marinated meat
===========================

Ingredient List
---------------

- 500g of meat, ribs or chicken wing
- Two tablespoon of korean spicy sauce
- Two tablespoon of olive oil
- One tablespoon of water
- One teaspoon of soya sauce
- One teaspoon of sesame oil
- Salt
- Pepper
- [optional] One tablespoon of chilly powder
- [optional] A bit of ginger
- [optional] A bit of smoke liquid

Steps
-----

1. Mix all the ingredients in a bowl except
2. Marinate the meat overnight
3. Cook the meat in oven
