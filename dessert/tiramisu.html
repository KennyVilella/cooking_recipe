Tiramisu

Ingredient List

  • 3 eggs

  • 100g of sugar canne

  • 10g of vanilla sugar

  • 250g of mascaporne

  • Biscuit

  • Coffee

  • Amaretto alcohol

Steps

  1. Separate yellow from white egg

  2. Mixed yellow egg with sugar until white

  3. Add mascaporne and mixed

  4. Whisk the white egg until solid and then mixed carefully the egg white to the mascaporne mixture
