Chicken Basquaise

Ingredient List

  • 2 Chicken legs

  • 4-6 Green peppers

  • 2x400g Tomato cans or 6-10 fresh tomatoes

  • 2-3 tablespoons of Oregano

  • [optional] Tomato concentrate

  • [optional] Chili powder

Steps

  1. Cook the chicken legs in olive oil until brown, remove

  2. Fry the green peppers

  3. Add the tomatoes and reduce fire, if fresh keep high fire for a few minutes

  4. Add oregano, salt, pepper, and optionally a bit of chili powder

  5. Keep for 15-20min, then add the chicken legs + Tomato concentrate and cover for around 15-20min
