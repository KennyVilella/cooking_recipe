.. _crepe:

Crepe
=====

Ingredient List
---------------

- 250g of flour
- 4 eggs
- 0.5L of milk
- 20g of melted butter
- 1 big spoon of sugar
- 1 big spoon of amaretto

Steps
-----

1. Mix the flour and eggs until homogeneous
2. Add progressively the milk
3. Add a pinch of salt, butter, sugar, amaretto, until homogeneous
4. Keep in fridge for at least one hour
