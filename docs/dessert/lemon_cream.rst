Lemon Cream
===========

Ingredient List
---------------

- 80g of butter
- 3 eggs
- 3 egg yolks
- 250ml water
- 250ml de lemon juice
- 200g of sugar
- 50g of corn starch or flour

Steps
-----

1. Put everything together in a pan at low heat
2. Keep mixing to avoid burning the cream
3. When the cream becomes solid, stop the heat
